FORSTANT Insights for Kailent

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Forstant Insights for Kailent ("Forstant Insights," "we," "our," or "us") is an analytics and reporting service designed to monitor available social media, website, audience, and content performance metrics.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed through the service, how that information is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Process

Depending on the integrations enabled and the permissions granted, Forstant Insights may process:

  • Account and profile information made available by connected platforms.
  • Available analytics, including audience counts, content views, engagement, likes, comments, and other performance metrics.
  • Website visits, content download events, and email subscription counts when the relevant integrations are implemented.
  • Historical metric snapshots and calculated growth statistics used to display trends over time.
  • Technical information needed to operate, protect, and troubleshoot the service.

The information actually processed depends on which integrations are configured and authorized. Not every listed data category is necessarily collected.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Display dashboards and performance reports.
  • Calculate weekly growth and historical trends.
  • Compare performance across connected platforms.
  • Maintain historical analytics records.
  • Maintain the security, reliability, and functionality of the service.
  • Identify trends and inform content and outreach decisions.

3. Pinterest API

Forstant Insights may connect to Pinterest through the Pinterest API after the relevant account authorization and access requirements have been satisfied.

If a Pinterest account is connected, we may process authorized account information, Pin information, and analytics that Pinterest makes available to the application. This information is used for analytics and reporting consistent with the permissions granted and applicable Pinterest terms.

Pinterest data is subject to Pinterest's policies and applicable API requirements. See Pinterest's Privacy Policy .

4. Other Third-Party Integrations

The service may integrate with platforms including YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, website analytics providers, and email subscription services. Information from these services is processed according to the permissions granted, functionality enabled, and applicable provider terms.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party providers. Their own privacy policies govern their services.

5. Access Tokens and Security

Certain integrations may require access tokens, API credentials, or other authorization information. These credentials should be handled through appropriate server-side configuration and security controls rather than exposed in public website code.

We aim to use reasonable security measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help host, operate, secure, or maintain Forstant Insights. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, protect rights, or address security issues.

We aim to limit information shared with service providers to what is necessary for their relevant functions.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Analytics records may be retained to support historical comparisons and growth reporting. Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

Users may revoke an integration's access through the relevant platform's account settings, where supported. Revoking access prevents future access as provided by that platform but may not automatically delete previously stored records.

To request deletion of information held by Forstant Insights, contact us using the details below. Requests will be handled subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Cookies and Technical Information

The website's hosting provider and related infrastructure may process technical information, such as request details and security logs, to deliver and protect the service. Any additional cookies or tracking technologies used by the deployed service should be disclosed and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

Forstant Insights is intended for authorized operators and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children through a public social networking service. If you believe personal information has been collected improperly, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the service, integrations, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page, with its effective date updated when appropriate.
